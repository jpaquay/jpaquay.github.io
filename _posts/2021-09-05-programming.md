A cozy chronological journey through thirty-five years of code, computers, paradigms, and stacks.

---

### 🕹️ 1991 — After-School Lessons & 8-Bit Hardware

{: .box-note}
**Commodore 64 & BASIC**

My very first programming experiences after school 🚸. Typing `POKE` & `PEEK` memory commands directly into `BASIC` on 8-bit Commodore 64 hardware.

* **Stack:** `BASIC`, `Commodore 64`

---

### 💻 1995 — Own Computer & Web Dawn

{: .box-note}
**Pentium 133MHz • 64MB RAM • MS-DOS & Windows**

Building my first personal scripts and early web experiments.

* **Automation & DBs:** `VBScript`, Office Macros, MS Access
* **Web & Interactive:** `HTML`, `CSS`, `JavaScript`, Macromedia Flash `ActionScript`
* **Backend Early Days:** `ASP.NET` (IIS / Access), moving from `WAMP` to `LAMP`, `PHP`, `MySQL`, `Bash`

---

### 🌐 1997 — Internet at Home

{: .box-note}
**56kbps Dial-Up Era 🏠**

Connecting home internet at 56kbps. Building full-stack web applications with LAMP, PHP, MySQL, and dynamic script automation.

---

### ⚙️ 1999 — Computer Science & Systems Engineering

{: .box-note}
**University CS Engineering**

Deep technical dive into low-level foundations, object-oriented systems, and numerical modeling.

* **Languages & Modeling:** `MATLAB`, `C`, `C++`, `Java`, `UML` architectural design
* **Systems & Data:** x86 `Assembler`, relational `SQL` database design

---

### 🐍 2006 — Network Automation & Systems Engineering

{: .box-note}
**Joined Cisco Systems**

Architecting mission-critical infrastructure for enterprise, EU institutions, and defense. Scripting router/switch plane behaviors and expanding into modern systems languages.

* **Network Automation:** Cisco IOS `TCL`
* **Modern Systems:** `Python`, `Rust`, `CoffeeScript`, server-side `Node.js`

---

### ☁️ 2018 — Google Cloud & Machine Learning

{: .box-note}
**Joined Google Cloud as Customer Engineer**

Designing scalable enterprise architectures on GCP.

* **Cloud & Distributed:** High-throughput `Go` services, `TypeScript`
* **Machine Learning:** `TensorFlow` with `Keras`
* **Cross-Platform:** `Flutter`

---

### 🤖 2021 — Post-Quantum Security & Scratch Education

{: .box-note}
**Crypto-Agility & Next-Gen Mentorship**

* **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):** Crypto-agility design, GKE container resilience, and quantum-safe security architectures.
* **Next Generation:** Teaching fundamental algorithmic logic and block programming to kids with `Scratch` 👾.

---

### ⚡ 2022 — Generative Pre-trained Transformers & LLMs

{: .box-note}
**The Deep Learning Tipping Point**

* Arrival of large-scale Transformer architectures (`BERT`, `GPT`, foundational `LLMs`).
* AI transition from isolated classifiers to generative reasoning partners.

---

### 🧠 2023 — Open-Weight Revolution & Local LLMs

{: .box-note}
**Sovereign Inference & Model Protocol Infrastructure**

* Open-weight ecosystem explosion (`Llama`, `Gemma`, `Ollama`).
* Running local GPU inference engines and Model Context Protocol (`MCP`) integrations 🚀.

---

### 🔮 2024 — Multimodal Gemini 1.5 & Vibe Coding

{: .box-note}
**Long Context & Multimodal Native Intelligence**

* Million-token context windows with `Gemini 1.5 Pro` & `Vertex AI`.
* Native audio/video reasoning and the emergence of fast, iterative *Vibe Coding* workflows ✨.

---

### 🐝 2025 — Agentic Revolution & Swarm Intelligence

{: .box-note}
**Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems**

* Transition from single-prompt chat to autonomous multi-agent swarms using Google's `ADK` (Agent Development Kit).
* Autonomous long-running code refactoring, automated security audits, and domain research swarms (`DeepSeek`, `Unsloth`) 👾.

---

### 🚀 2026+ — Gemini 3.5 & Autonomous AI Engineering

{: .box-note}
**SRE Co-Piloting & Self-Healing Cloud**

* Full pair programming & SRE co-piloting with Google Antigravity & `Gemini 3.5`.
* Serverless multi-agent topologies operating zero-downtime, self-healing cloud architectures with active chaos resilience 🛡️.
